\section*{Rate model}

The CSV gives $F(E)$ in $(\mathrm{m}^2\,\mathrm{sr}\,\mathrm{s}\,\mathrm{GeV})^{-1}$,
with $E$ in eV. These illustrative primary intensities are digitized from
Sven Lafebre's graphic (CC BY-SA 3.0). See \texttt{DATA\_PROVENANCE.md} and
\texttt{NOTICE} for attribution. Physical uncertainty is unquantified;
the plotted energy convention is retained without resolving species or
kinetic-versus-total energy distinctions.

For a one-sided horizontal surface of area $A$ under an isotropic downward
intensity, the rate in events per second is
\[
R_{\mathrm{CSV}}(>E_{\mathrm{th}};E_{\max})
=
\pi A_{\mathrm{m}^2}\,10^{-9}
\int_{E_{\mathrm{th}}}^{E_{\max}} F(E)\,dE_{\mathrm{eV}} .
\]
Here $E_{\max}\simeq3.36\times10^{20}\,\mathrm{eV}$ is the CSV endpoint.
The integral uses power-law interpolation between tabulated points;
$10^{-9}$ converts eV to GeV and $\pi$ is the projected solid-angle factor.
For $A=\SI{1}{km^2}$ the prefactor is $\pi\times10^{-3}$.

The default omits flux beyond $E_{\max}$. The optional \texttt{last-segment}
model extrapolates the final slope $\gamma_{\mathrm{last}}<-1$, adding
\[
\int_{E_{\max}}^{\infty} F(E)\,dE
=
\frac{F(E_{\max})E_{\max}}{-\left(\gamma_{\mathrm{last}}+1\right)} .
\]
The tail is model dependent and matters near the upper endpoint.
Atmospheric showers, detector response, solar modulation, and geomagnetic
cutoffs are not modeled. A scalar aperture in $\si{m^2.sr}$ can replace $\pi A$.

\section*{Piecewise approximation}

The fit covers $10^{10}$--$10^{20}\,\mathrm{eV}$ for the bundled CSV with
truncation. Its five pieces share fitted breakpoint rates for continuity.
Calibration minimizes squared log-rate residuals at the endpoints and logarithmic
quarter points of each interval, counting shared endpoints once.
